dream edges 2024

Video, AI

As part of Dream (of) Land at Spazju Kreattiv.

‘dream edges’ invites the use of artificial intelligence to imagine and dream beyond the maltese landscape. this dual video installation, rooted in the Maltese coastline and seascape, transcends traditional boundaries of landscape art. Through the fusion of personal film photography and AI, the artwork fabricates ethereal landscapes that challenge our perceptions of the natural world.

In this work, AI is more than a tool - it is a collaborator that redefines the familiar, suggesting alternate realms shaped by memory and digital alchemy. ‘dream edges’ is a conceptual exploration, urging viewers to contemplate the fluidity of reality and the transformative power of dreams.
